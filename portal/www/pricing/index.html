Pricing价格

One account, many models. You pay for what you use, from a prepaid balance. v1 is a prepaid top-up product, not an automatic-renewing subscription.

How billing works

  • Per-token usage. Text models are charged by the tokens you send and receive; voice and other tools by their own usage units. Your balance and usage are shown in the app. [CONFIRM wording and units]
  • Prepaid balance. Add balance first; usage is deducted as you go.
  • Bring your own key (“Refuel”). If your balance runs low you can connect your own model key and keep working; those requests are billed by your provider, not by us.

Ways to top up

  • Card — paid in AUD on Stripe’s secure page. PayPal is not part of v1.
  • USDT — on the networks listed in the top-up screen on the home page.
  • Codes — see /redeem.

Rewards

Revival cards can reset a used-up allowance and are earned through top-up bonuses and referrals: when a friend enters your referral code and makes their first top-up, you each receive one card. [CONFIRM current bonus tiers]

如何计费

  • 按 token 计费。文本模型按你发送和接收的 token 计费；语音和其他工具按各自的用量单位计费。你的余额和用量可在应用中查看。[待确认：措辞与单位]
  • 预付余额。先充值，用量随用随扣。
  • 自带密钥（“Refuel”）。余额不足时，你可以接入自己的模型密钥继续使用；这些请求由你的提供商计费，而不是由我们计费。

充值方式

  • 银行卡——在 Stripe 的安全页面以澳元支付。PayPal 不在 v1 范围内。
  • USDT——使用首页充值界面中列出的网络。
