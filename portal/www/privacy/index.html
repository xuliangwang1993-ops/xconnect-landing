This policy explains what information X-Connect AI collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. “X-Connect”, “we” and “us” mean A.C.N. 688 027 367 PTY. LTD. (ABN 37 688 027 367), Australia.

1. What we handle

  • Account information — your email address and the sign-in identifiers created when you use email, Apple, Google or a passkey. Sign-in is provided through Supabase.
  • Your conversations and files — the messages, attachments and voice audio you send to an AI model. If you use chat sync, conversations are also stored on our servers so they appear on your other devices. [CONFIRM exactly which data is synced, and whether sync is on by default]
  • Usage and billing records — which model you used, token counts, your balance and top-up history, revival cards and referral records. We keep these to charge correctly and prevent abuse.
  • Device identifier — an app/device identifier used so the app can work (for example, to apply referral and top-up rules). It is not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • Optional device data — location, camera, microphone, photos, calendar, reminders and Apple Health are only accessed if you grant permission and use a feature that needs them. Anything a feature reads becomes part of the request you send to the AI model you chose. [CONFIRM per-feature behaviour and what, if anything, is stored on our servers]
  • Your own API keys (“Refuel”) — if you connect your own model key, it is used for your requests only. [CONFIRM where the key is stored: on device only, or on our servers, and how it is protected]

2. Why we use it

To provide the service (sign you in, route your request to a model, sync your chats, run voice), to bill you and apply top-ups, to keep the service secure and prevent fraud or abuse, to fix problems, and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

  • AI model providers. X-Connect is a gateway: what you type, attach or say is sent to the model provider that serves your request (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, DeepSeek or others), possibly through a model-routing provider. Those providers process your data under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not send information you are not comfortable sharing with them.
